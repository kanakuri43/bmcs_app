Release a Monthly Closing (Administrator)

When to use this
Use this to cancel a monthly closing when you confirmed it by mistake, or when you need to correct sales or receipts for a month that has already been closed.
Once released, you can immediately register, correct, or cancel sales and receipts for that month.
This screen is for administrators only. It appears in the menu only for employees with permission level 9.

1.Choose the month to release

From the main menu, open Monthly Closing Release (月次締め解除処理) under Monthly (月次). Enter the Year (年) as 4 digits and choose the Closing Month (集計年月). The confirmed results for all customers for that month are listed.

  1. Year (年): Enter 4 digits.
  2. Closing Month (集計年月): Choose from the months closed in that year.
  3. The items to be released. All customers for that month are released together (you cannot select customers and release only some of them).
  4. Release (解除実行) (F8): Choose Yes (はい) in the confirmation message to release.

2.When a release is not possible

  • If a later month has already been confirmed for that customer, you cannot release the month. Release months in order, newest first (for example, release October before September).
  • If even one customer cannot be released, the reason appears in the Remarks (備考) column of the list and Release (解除実行) cannot be pressed (nothing is released for any customer).

3.What to do after releasing

  1. Release with Release (解除実行) (F8).
  2. Correct the sales and receipts (you can register, correct, or cancel them right away).
  3. Close the same month again in Monthly Closing (月次締め処理) (the released results are overwritten with the new values).
