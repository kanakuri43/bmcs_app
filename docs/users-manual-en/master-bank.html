Register Bank Accounts (Bank Master)

When to use this
Register your company's accounts that receive payments. The registered accounts are used in these two places.
・When choosing the receiving account (入金先口座) for a bank transfer in Receipt Entry (入金入力)
・When choosing the transfer destination printed on the invoice in the Customer Master (得意先マスタ) (up to 2 per customer)

1.Register an account

From the main menu, open Bank Master (銀行マスタ) under Master Management (マスタ管理). Enter the bank account code (銀行口座コード) and press Enter (if it is already registered, its details appear; if not, continue entering the details as a new registration).

  1. Bank account code (銀行口座コード): Required. A management code that your company assigns.
  2. Bank name and branch name (銀行名・支店名): Printed as the transfer destination on invoices.
  3. Account type (口座種別): Choose Ordinary (普通), Checking (当座), and so on.
  4. Account number (口座番号).
  5. Account holder (口座名義): Enter the name (in kana) exactly as it appears in the passbook.
  6. Display order (表示順): The order in which accounts appear in lists and choices (smaller numbers come first).
  7. Press Save (保存) to register. When you stop using an account, deactivate (無効化) it (past receipts remain).
