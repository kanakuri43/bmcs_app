Register a Customer (Customer Master / 得意先マスタ)

When to use this
Use this screen to register a new customer, to correct an address or person in charge, or to stop using a customer you no longer do business with.
Orders, sales, and receipts can only be entered for customers registered here.
The following items cannot be changed after you register the customer. Check them carefully before you save.
・Transaction type (取引条件) (per-transaction / closing-cycle), and the closing day (締日) and tax unit (税区分) that go with it
・Rounding method (端数区分) (how fractions of consumption tax are handled: round down / round off / round up)

1.Open the screen and enter a customer code

In the main menu, open Master Management (マスタ管理) (it starts out collapsed, so click it to expand), then open Customer Master (得意先マスタ).

  • To register a new customer: Enter a new code in the Customer Code (得意先コード) field and press Enter. The message "未登録です。新規登録として入力してください" (Not registered. Please enter as a new registration) appears. Just keep entering the details.
  • To edit an existing customer: Enter the code and press Enter, or press Space to search and select. The saved details appear. Make your changes and save.

2.Enter the fields

The example below registers a closing-cycle customer (締め取引).

  1. Customer code (得意先コード): Required. Decide the number yourself (you cannot use a number that another customer already has).
  2. Customer name, kana, address, etc. (得意先名・カナ・住所など): The customer name is required. It is printed on invoices and delivery notes. The kana is used for searching.
  3. Sales employee code (営業担当社員コード): Optional. If you enter one, it becomes the default person in charge for orders and sales (press Space to search).
  4. Transaction type (取引条件): Choose Per-transaction (都度取引) (customers such as government offices and schools, who are billed for each transaction) or Closing-cycle (締め取引) (customers who are billed in a monthly batch). This cannot be changed after registration.
  5. If you choose Closing-cycle, the fields for Closing day (締日) (1 to 31; use 99 for the last day of the month) and Tax unit (税区分) (per invoice / per slip) appear. Per-transaction customers are handled with consumption tax included in the amounts (tax-inclusive, per line item).
  6. Billing customer code (請求得意先コード): Enter this only when you want to bill on behalf of another customer as well (example: to combine a branch's billing into the head office, enter the head office's code on the branch). If left blank, the customer is billed directly.
  7. Rounding method (端数区分): How fractions of consumption tax are handled (round down / round off / round up). This cannot be changed after registration.
  8. Press Save (保存) to register. To stop entering and clear the screen, press Cancel (キャンセル). To stop using a customer you no longer do business with, press Deactivate (無効化); the customer can no longer be used on new slips (past slips are kept).

Other fields

FieldDescription
Print representative's name on invoices (請求書に代表者印字する)If checked, our company's representative name is printed on invoices addressed to this customer.
Bank account for payment on invoices, up to 2 (請求書の振込先口座（最大2件）)The bank accounts printed on the invoice for payment. Press Space in the code field and choose from the accounts registered beforehand in Bank Master (銀行マスタ). If left blank, nothing is printed.
Even if you want a different addressee name for each school class or teacher, you do not need to add more customers. Just rewrite the name field when you enter the slip (for example in Order Entry (受注入力) or Itemized Invoice (明細請求書)).
