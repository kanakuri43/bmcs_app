Register Company Information (Administrator)

When to use this
Register your company's name, address, and registration number, which are printed on invoices and itemized invoices. Register them once at the start; after that, change them only when your company name or address changes.
Invoices must contain the items required of a qualified invoice (適格請求書) under Japan's invoice system (such as the registration number), so if this screen is left empty, your invoices will not be correct.
This screen is for administrators only. It appears in the menu only for employees with permission level 9. There is only one company information record (you do not need to call it up by code; you can start typing as soon as it opens).

1.Enter the information and save

From the main menu, open Company Information (自社情報) under Master Management (マスタ管理) (it is collapsed at first, so click it to expand). If information has already been registered, it is shown as it is.

  1. Company name (会社名): Printed as the issuer of the invoice.
  2. Registration number (登録番号): The registration number of a qualified invoice issuer ("T" followed by 13 digits). It is printed on invoices and itemized invoices.
  3. Postal code, address, phone, FAX (郵便番号・住所・電話・FAX): Printed as the issuer of the invoice.
  4. Representative name (代表者名): Printed on invoices addressed to customers whose Customer Master (得意先マスタ) has "Print representative on invoice (請求書に代表者印字する)" turned on.
  5. Save (保存): Your changes do not take effect unless you press this.
